## Working rules and evidence

The owner requested autonomous continuation: after each validated slice, update evidence, commit, push and open a stacked draft PR, then inspect and continue the next task without waiting for another prompt or renewed architecture approval. Preserve a durable truthful checkpoint if usage, environment availability or a genuine authorization boundary prevents work. A five-hour continuation automation was enabled in the assistant platform; it attempts resumption but does not establish the actual quota reset time or guarantee environment availability. Keep the existing merge, public deployment, paid activation and production-integration restrictions in force.

Use synthetic data until a phase explicitly authorizes a live integration. Do not merge or deploy publicly as part of ordinary implementation. Record command, result and limitations with each completed task. Keep provider/runtime/tool boundaries replaceable and policy failures closed. Phase 1 must meet the acceptance criteria in `docs/research/phase-0-validation.md`; passing unit tests alone is not completion.
